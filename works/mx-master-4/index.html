Project info

Engineered for flow.

Logitech MX Master 4 is a premium productivity mouse with a high-performance electromagnetic scroll wheel and smart action ring. Equipped with an 8K all-surface sensor and dual-mode scrolling, it delivers quiet, ergonomic comfort for all-day use. It supports smooth multi-device switching and file transfer, with long battery life and durable build to greatly enhance office and creative efficiency.

罗技 MX Master 4，智能提效旗舰办公鼠标，让办公效率翻倍。搭载智能行动环、8K 全表面追踪传感器与双模式电磁滚轮；静音舒适的人体工学设计贴合掌心，支持多设备无缝切换与文件直传，长续航且耐用，大幅提升办公创作效率。
