Multi-functional Phone Holder

Project info

One object. Multiple modes.

This magnetic phone holder features strong MagSafe-compatible magnets for quick, secure attachment, even with non-MagSafe phones. Its foldable tripod design supports multiple modes for viewing and shooting. Made of lightweight yet durable aluminum, it includes expansion ports for photography accessories, making it a portable, all-in-one solution.

这款磁吸手机支架，强磁适配 MagSafe / 非 MagSafe 机型，一秒吸附不掉落。可折叠三脚架设计，支持多形态切换，追剧、拍摄都适用。全铝材质轻巧耐用，自带扩展接口，轻松兼容摄影配件，是便携又实用的全能支架。
