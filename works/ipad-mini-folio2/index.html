设计目标

基于产品功能与材质特性，构建具有情绪表达的视觉语言体系。在保证电商传播效率的前提下，强化产品“轻薄 + 科技 + 质感”的核心认知，同时提升品牌审美与差异化表现。

Visual Strategy / 视觉策略

For the overall visual construction, we adopt a “Product-led + Atmosphere-assisted” approach.

Primary Visual Layer

Strengthen the display of the phone case itself, using light and shadow gradients to simulate “sunset afterglow” and highlight the detailed layers of the woven texture.

Material Expression Layer

Magnify the microscopic structure of aramid fibers, enhance the three-dimensional sense through macro photography and oblique lighting, and establish a cognitive contrast that “technical materials ≠ coldness”.

With a lightweight 142g construction, the MagEZ Folio 2 is lighter than 95% of iPad cases that serve as stands.
